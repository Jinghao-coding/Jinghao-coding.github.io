Jinghao Wang 王靖皓

I am a PhD candidate in Software Engineering at Beihang University, advised by Prof. Chunming Hu. Previously, I worked as a software engineer at ByteDance.

My research focuses on GPU scheduling and LLM systems. I study performance prediction, resource management in shared clusters, and the execution of multi-agent LLM workflows.
